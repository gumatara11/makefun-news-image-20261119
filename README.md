One image summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026, as a newspaper front page: 50 sourced stories, the day's biggest headlines up top, the rest in brief below.

Design: masthead "THE WORLD" with the date, lead story on the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen with a drawn dusk-skyline photo band and the joint declaration quote, second row on the ICJ genocide verdict and Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO, 12 illustrated briefs under "Around the World", and 35 one-liners under "In Brief".

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` writes `out/entry.png`.
